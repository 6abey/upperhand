privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026

upperhand is an iPhone app that reads handwriting and gestures from a wristband, or from your screen, and answers questions. It's made by Gabriel Ascencio ("we"). This page explains what upperhand keeps, where it keeps it, and what leaves your iPhone.

The short version

  • There's no upperhand account and no upperhand server. We don't receive your questions, your answers or anything about how you use the app.
  • By default, answers are made on your iPhone.
  • If you turn on smarter answers (Pro) with your own OpenAI API key, the question you wrote is sent to OpenAI under your key.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no selling or sharing of personal information.

Your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or computer) may include upperhand's history and settings, as they do for any app. Apple's privacy policy covers those backups.

What leaves your iPhone, and only when you use the feature

Answers in the cloud (Pro, optional). With smarter answers on and your own OpenAI API key saved, upperhand sends the text of the question you wrote, with fixed instructions, from your iPhone directly to OpenAI (api.openai.com), under your OpenAI account. upperhand asks you once before the first question goes to OpenAI, and asks OpenAI not to store the response. OpenAI says API data isn't used to train its models unless you opt in, and that abuse-monitoring logs are kept for up to 30 days (OpenAI: your data). OpenAI's privacy policy governs what OpenAI keeps. We never see the request or the answer. Turn smarter answers off, or remove the key, and nothing goes to OpenAI.

Data leaves your iPhone only for services you turn on, and each of those services handles it under its own privacy policy.

Pairing your wristband (once). Your wristband accepts only an owner its maker has enrolled. If you pair the band on your iPhone, upperhand shows the maker's own sign-in page inside the app, in a private web view. upperhand doesn't read or keep your password. upperhand receives a session token, keeps it in the keychain, and sends it with your band's public key, your account ID at the maker and an app-made device ID to the maker's pairing service to register your iPhone as the band's owner. After that, the band and your iPhone talk directly over Bluetooth. The maker's privacy policy covers the sign-in and the pairing service.

Purchases. Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Crash reports. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. We use them only to fix bugs.

Calendar (honor mode, optional)

If you allow it, honor mode reads the titles and times of upcoming events on your iPhone to suggest windows when answers switch off (for example an event named "midterm"). This happens on your iPhone. Nothing from your calendar leaves it, and nothing turns on without your confirmation.

What we don't do

No advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of personal information. upperhand doesn't use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Bluetooth is used only to talk to your wristband. Music access is used only to play, pause and skip songs when you assign those actions to gestures.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Clear your history: Settings › privacy › answer history › clear.
  • Remove everything upperhand keeps (band key, sign-in, API key, history, settings): Settings › privacy › delete everything on this iPhone. Your subscription stays with your Apple Account.
  • Or one at a time: remove your API key in Settings › answers › API key; forget your band on the band tab; sign out of the wristband maker on the band tab › pair › sign out.
  • Deleting the app removes its storage, but keychain items stay on the iPhone. To remove the band key, the sign-in and the API key, use delete everything before you delete the app.
  • Cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

We hold no personal data about you, so there's nothing for us to delete. For questions or requests, email gabeasce@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Children

upperhand isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. We collect none from anyone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal data, and to object to its use. Because we don't receive or keep personal data, we have none to provide. Data held by OpenAI or by the wristband's maker is covered by their own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date. If a change means data leaves your iPhone in a new way, the app asks you first.
