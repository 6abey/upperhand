support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email gabeasce@gmail.com. You'll hear back within a few days.

Don't have a wristband?

You can still use upperhand on your iPhone alone: write or type a question on the write tab, tap ask, and see the answer on screen with a matching tap pattern from your iPhone.

My band won't connect

Open the band tab and use troubleshoot: reset to gestures, pair again, or forget the band and pair it fresh. Make sure Bluetooth is on and upperhand is allowed to use it in iPhone Settings.

Restore a purchase

Open settings › upperhand pro › restore purchases. Subscriptions belong to your Apple Account, so use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › upperhand pro.

Honor mode

Honor mode switches answers off for a window you choose, for times when answers don't belong. Ending it early takes a 10-minute wait.

Delete your data

Everything upperhand keeps stays on your iPhone. Settings › reset upperhand removes it. See the privacy policy for details.
