knowledge at your fingertips.

upperhand is a study and preparation companion for students, professionals and anyone learning something new. Write a question on any surface with your finger, and feel the answer as a few quiet buzzes on an EMG wristband, or see it on your iPhone.

Answers run on your iPhone by default. Honor mode switches answers off for a set window, for times when they don't belong.

upperhand is an independent app. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Meta or any wristband maker.
